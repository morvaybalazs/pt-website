Personal training in Edinburgh

One-to-one sessions with a qualified personal trainer and BSc (Hons) graduate: at your home gym or my private garage gym.

I'm Balazs Morvay, a personal trainer and sports massage therapist based in East Calder, just west of Edinburgh, with a Physical Activity and Health BSc (Hons) and more than 10 years in the industry. I keep in-person sessions small and personal, and I also offer sports massage with a plan to take home and online coaching.

I work mainly with busy professionals who want to get stronger, lose fat or get back into training. That includes people taking GLP-1 weight-loss medicines, who are very welcome.

Common questions

Do I need to be a coaching client to book a session?

No. You can book single sessions as you need them. If you'd like a plan to follow between sessions, you can add online coaching.

Where in Edinburgh do sessions happen?

At your home gym, or at my private garage gym in East Calder (I share the exact location when you book). Home visits cover Edinburgh and nearby, subject to availability.

Do I need my own equipment?

No. If you don't have a home gym, we can train at my garage gym. If you'd like to set up your own space, home gym planning is included with coaching.

Can I combine in-person sessions with online coaching?

Yes. Online coaching covers your week-to-week plan, check-ins and nutrition, and in-person sessions are there for technique work whenever they help.
