Personal Training in Edinburgh

One-to-one training with a BSc (Hons) qualified personal trainer, based in Edinburgh. At your own home gym or my private garage gym - no crowds, no memberships, no contracts.

I'm Balazs Morvay, a personal trainer based in Edinburgh with a Physical Activity and Health BSc (Hons), a sports massage qualification, and 10+ years in the industry. Most of my coaching happens online, which means in-person sessions here in Edinburgh stay small in number and personal - you're not one of thirty clients being shuffled through a commercial gym floor.

I work mainly with busy professionals: people who want to lose weight, get stronger, and keep the results without organising their life around the gym. That includes specialist support for people on or coming off GLP-1 medications (Ozempic, Wegovy, Mounjaro) who want to protect their muscle while the weight comes off.

Common questions

Where in Edinburgh do sessions happen?

Two options: I come to your own home gym, or you train at my private garage gym in Edinburgh - the exact location is shared when you book. Mobile sessions cover Edinburgh and nearby, subject to availability.

How much do in-person sessions cost?

In-person sessions are £60 each, booked as needed with no minimum commitment. Mobile sports massage is £50 per 45-minute session. Full online coaching is £200/month, or a one-off bespoke 4-12 week programme block is £100.

Do I need my own equipment?

No. If you don't have a home gym, you can train at my private garage gym. If you'd like to set up your own space, home gym planning is included with coaching - there's also a free home gym guide.

Can I combine in-person training with online coaching?

Yes - that's how most Edinburgh clients work with me: online coaching for the week-to-week programme, check-ins, and nutrition guidance, with in-person sessions for technique work whenever useful.
