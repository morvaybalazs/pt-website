Training check-in

Review your training, food, recovery and progress in about 3 minutes. These are the same questions my coaching clients answer at each check-in.

How to use it: fill it in on the same day every week, or every two weeks. Tick one answer for each question. Nothing you write here is sent anywhere: print it, or save it as a PDF and write on it. Look for patterns over 4 to 6 weeks, not single weeks.
