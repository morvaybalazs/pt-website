The session

75 minutes, and a plan to take home

A Massage and Plan session always has the same five parts, so you know what you're paying for.

  1. A talk and a movement check about 15 minutes

    What you do, what's bothering you and what you want to get back to. Then a few simple movements, like a squat and a reach overhead, to see what's tight and what moves well.

  2. The massage 45 minutes

    Sports massage on the areas the check pointed to, at a pressure you're comfortable with. Only the area I'm working on is uncovered.

  3. Two or three exercises about 15 minutes

    We go through each one until you're confident with it. At my garage gym we can use weights. At home, a band or your body weight.

  4. Your plan, the same day

    The exercises written up with sets, reps and how often to do them, and sent to your phone, so you don't have to remember them.

  5. A check-in a few days later

    I message to ask how you feel and to answer any questions about the exercises.

I don't diagnose injuries. If something needs a physio or a GP, I'll tell you and suggest where to go.

  1. 1Calf raise, slow on the way down3 sets of 15, every other day
  2. 2Kneeling hip flexor stretch2 x 45 seconds each side, daily

Who it's for

For people who train, and people who sit

Most people come with tight spots and aches, not injuries.

You run, lift or play sport

Calves before a race, hips after heavy squats, a shoulder that complains on the bench. A massage around a big week, and exercises that fit the training you already do.

You sit at a desk all day

A stiff neck, tight shoulders, a lower back that aches by Friday. The massage eases it, and a few minutes of exercises during the week give you something to do about it.

The same spot keeps coming back

You have regular massages and the same area tightens up again. We look at your training and your week, not just the sore spot, and build your exercises around what we find.

Questions

Good to know

Do I need an injury to book?

No. Most people come with tight spots, soreness from training or aches from sitting. If you have a recent injury, mention it when you book. I don't diagnose injuries, and if something needs a physio or a GP, I'll tell you.

How firm is the massage?

As firm as you're comfortable with. Sports massage can feel strong in places. Tell me if it's too much and I'll ease off straight away.

What should I wear?

Shorts and a loose top you can move in, for the movement check and the exercises. During the massage, only the area I'm working on is uncovered, and the rest of you stays under a towel.

Where do sessions happen?

At your home, in Edinburgh and nearby. I bring the massage couch and need a clear space about the size of a single bed, with room to walk around it. Or at my private garage gym in Edinburgh, where we can use weights for the exercises. I share the exact address when you book.

Is there any reason I shouldn't have a massage?

Sometimes. A fever, a recent injury or operation, a blood clot, blood-thinning medicine, some skin conditions and pregnancy can each mean waiting, changing the massage or checking with your doctor first. Once we've agreed a time, I send you a short private health form that asks about these.

How do payment and cancelling work?

You pay for each session, as agreed when you book. There are no packages and no subscription. Please give at least 24 hours' notice to move or cancel. With less notice, I may charge up to the price of the session.
