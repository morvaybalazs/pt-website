Your first visit

60 minutes, and a plan for afterwards

We start by talking and watching how you move, so the massage goes where you need it. We finish with two or three exercises, so you leave feeling looser and knowing what to do next.

  1. We talk, then I watch you move about 10 minutes

    Tell me about your training, your work and anything that's been bothering you. Then a few simple movements, like a squat and an overhead reach, show me what's tight and what's moving well. That decides where the massage goes.

  2. Sports massage, where you need it about 40 minutes

    Focused work on the areas the movement check pointed to. The pressure is always your call, and you stay covered by towels apart from the area I'm working on.

  3. Exercises, coached in the session about 10 minutes

    Two or three exercises chosen for what we found, practised together until you're confident doing them on your own. A band or your body weight is plenty.

  4. Your plan on your phone, the same day

    Each exercise written up with sets, reps and how often to do it, so you never have to remember what I showed you.

  5. A check-in a few days later

    I'll message to see how you're feeling and how the exercises are going, and to answer any questions that have come up.

I don't diagnose injuries, but if anything we find needs a physio or a GP to look at it, I'll tell you straight away and suggest where to go.

Questions

Before you book

Do I need an injury to book?

Not at all. Tight spots, soreness from training and aches from sitting are all good reasons to book. If you have a recent injury, mention it when you book. I don't diagnose injuries, and if something needs a physio or a GP, I'll tell you.

How firm is the massage?

As firm as you're comfortable with, and never more. Sports massage can feel strong in places, so just tell me if it's too much and I'll ease off straight away.

What should I wear?

Shorts and a loose top you can move in, for the movement check and the exercises. During the massage, only the area I'm working on is uncovered, and the rest of you stays under a towel.

Where does the massage happen?

At your home, anywhere in Edinburgh and West Lothian, and travel is included in the price. I bring the massage couch, and just need a clear space about the size of a single bed with room to walk around it.

Is there any reason I shouldn't have a massage?

Occasionally, yes. A fever, a recent injury or operation, a blood clot, blood-thinning medicine, some skin conditions and pregnancy can each mean waiting, changing the massage or checking with your doctor first. At the start of your first visit, you fill in a short private health form with me that asks about these.

How do payment and cancelling work?

You pay for each session, in the way we agree when you book, with no packages or subscription. If you need to move or cancel, just let me know as early as you can. With less than 24 hours' notice, I may charge up to the price of the session.
